# Version 2 of the plan

Written by Claude chat, 17 Nov 2026. Read this after `CLAUDE.md`. **Where an older page (`DESIGN.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `PLAN.md`, `CONTENT.md`, the mockups) disagrees with this one, this one wins.** The older pages stay true for everything this page does not mention.

## What changed

The app is no longer only yoga lessons. It becomes one small daily practice: **move, breathe, sit, write**. Maria's lessons stay the heart of it; breathing exercises, meditation and a private journal (free pages and three guided journals) join them. The look gains a light theme and more colour.

The owner has used apps that do all of this badly: cluttered, a million choices, nothing you want to come back to for five quiet minutes. So one rule sits above every other in this app: **calm beats complete.** When in doubt, show less.

Five tabs, in this order, and never a sixth:

| Practice | `(tabs)/practice.tsx` | Lessons and series; Maria's notes at the bottom | `area.move` |

Names that moved:
- **Maria's tips and notes** were the "Journal" tab. They are now called **Notes** wherever a person or a file name shows them: the pages `/notes` and `/notes/[slug]`, the part `NoteCard`. Only the data keeps its old name, because it is permanent: the folder `content/journal/` and the key `journal` in `content.json`. In code, "journal" now means the person's own journal, except in those two data names.
- **Studio** is no longer a tab. Maria's page lives at `/maria` and is opened from the billboard.
- **My practice** is now **You**, at `/me`.

## One day, four small things

- Today (the lower part of Home) offers **one** suggestion for each area that exists: Move, Breathe, Sit, Write. One card each, one tap each, never a list to choose from. Browsing is what the tabs are for.
- A day counts (its candle is lit) as soon as **any one** of the four is done.
- When something is finished, its closing screen may offer **one** next step ("Three minutes of breath?") as a quiet button. Never a menu.
- One reminder a day at most, for the whole practice. Never one per area.
- What is done in one area is known in the others without asking (the health check-in already knows that a lesson was done today).

## Limits that keep it calm

These are hard limits. A brief may ask for less, never for more.

1. Each tab opens on a title, **one** suggested thing, then **one** short shelf. One gold button per screen.
2. Choices are capped: at most 6 breathing exercises, 3 lengths for each, 8 meditations before "See all", exactly 3 guided journals plus the free pages.
3. No filter and no search on a list of fewer than 8 items.
4. Nothing is shown empty. An area with nothing in it is hidden in the production app (the preview app may show a quiet placeholder).
5. The science ("Why this works") is always one tap away, never on the main surface. It says in plain words how strong the evidence is, including when it is weak. No health promise anywhere ("relieves", "cures", "treats").
6. No pop-up when the app opens. No badge, no red dot, no points, no levels. The candles are the only reward.
7. The person's journal never leaves the phone. Not to Supabase, not as a count, not to anyone.

## The look, version 2

Still black and gold: a near-black ground, ivory text, and gold as the one colour of action. What is new:

- **Light and dark.** Two palettes with the same names in `src/theme/tokens.ts` (`palette.dark`, `palette.light`). The app follows the phone by default; You → Appearance offers System, Light, Dark. No screen and no part imports a colour: they read it with `useTheme()`.
- **Colour by area.** Each area owns one colour (table above). It tints the active tab, the overlines and small icons inside that area, the area's card on Today (`areaSoft.*` is the card's ground), and its auras. Gold stays the only filled button, in every area.
- **Covers are pictures.** Auras and photos look the same in both themes. Words and icons on top of a cover use `onCover.*`, never `text.*`: ink on a night scene cannot be read.
- **The room dims.** Some places are always dark, whatever the theme: the billboard, the welcome, every player (video, breath, meditation) and every closing screen. Wrap them in `<ThemeScope scheme="dark">`.
- **Gold on a light ground.** As a word or an icon it is `accent.gold` (a bronze in the light palette). As a fill it is `accent.fill` with a hairline of `accent.fillBorder`. Never bright gold text on ivory.
- **Five new auras**: `dawn`, `tide`, `iris`, `rose`, `saffron`. By area: lessons keep the first five; Breathe `tide`; Meditate `iris`; Journal `rose`; Home and Maria `saffron`. Content written without an `aura` still gets one of the first five (`autoAuraNames`), so existing covers do not change.
- No shadows, in either theme. Depth is a lighter surface plus a hairline.
- Type, spacing, radius and motion are unchanged.

## The new areas, in short

Each gets its own brief when its phase comes. Until then, build nothing for them and lay no groundwork.

- **Journal.** *Free pages*: a blank page, with an optional "Give me a prompt". *Guided journals*: Gratitude, Habit, Health. One engine draws all three from content: a journal is a list of pages, a page is a list of prompts of a few kinds (text, short list, choice, scale, yes or no, fill in the blanks, a reminder of an earlier answer, a reading card). One page a day per journal; missing a day costs nothing; you continue where you stopped. Everything written stays on the phone.
- **Breathe.** An exercise is content: a named sequence of steps (in, hold, out) with their seconds. The check refuses fast breathing. One player: an aura that swells and settles, a word, a count, and a soft tick in the hand so that it works with eyes closed.
- **Meditate.** A timer with a bell, and guided sittings. A sitting is content: Maria's recorded voice when there is one, with its lines as captions; without a recording, the lines appear in silence with a soft chime.
- **Today** learns each area as it arrives.

## Decisions (17 Nov 2026)

- The app grows from yoga lessons to a daily practice: move, breathe, sit, write. Calm beats complete.
- Five tabs: Home, Practice, Breathe, Meditate, Journal. Studio becomes Maria's page behind the billboard. Maria's tips and notes are renamed Notes.
- Home opens on a billboard for Maria and her class, once a day; Today is under it.
- Light and dark themes; colour by area; gold stays the colour of action.
- The journal is private: on the phone only, never sent, not even counted.
- The guided journals are written fresh for this app from published research. Nothing is copied or adapted from an existing journal or app, and no other product is named in the app or on its store page.
- Nowhere in the app: fast breathing, a breath hold longer than 8 seconds, weight, calories or body measurements.
- Meditations wait for Maria's voice; until then, a timer and silent captions. No stock music at launch; the bells are made by a script in the repository.

## Decisions (17 Nov 2026, evening)

- **The builder gets more freedom** (`CLAUDE.md`, "How much freedom you have"): it keeps Expo current within its SDK, may add a library within clear limits, fixes the bugs it finds, and corrects plain factual mistakes in protected files. The briefs say what and why; the how is the builder's. Decisions still belong to the architect and the owner.
- **The briefs of Phases 3 and 4 were written together.** Each is still built in its own Claude Code session and its own pull request.
- **Today's big card** (the Move suggestion) is: the next lesson of a series started, not finished, and practised in the last 7 days; else the featured lesson; else the newest. There is no separate "Continue" block on Today.
- **The record of the day** lives in `src/day/` (`ywm:days:v1`): one entry per thing done, `move` for a lesson; Breathe, Meditate and Journal will write into it. Saved lessons and answers live in `src/practice/` (`ywm:practice:v1`).
- **The privacy page says only what the app does at that point.** Each later phase that starts sending or keeping something adds its paragraph (Journal in Phase 5, answers in Phase 9, notifications in Phase 10).
- **The public app never asks Expo for updates**, and the website loads no fonts from Google: nothing reaches a third party that the privacy page does not name.
- **Practice follows limit 3**: the search field and the filters appear only from 8 lessons.
