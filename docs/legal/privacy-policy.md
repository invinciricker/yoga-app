# Privacy

*Last updated: {updated}*

{appName} is a free app of yoga lessons and quiet daily practice by {teacher}. This page says, in plain words, what the app knows about you. The short answer: almost nothing.

## No account, no name

You never sign up. The app does not ask for your name, your email address or your phone number, and it does not read your contacts, your location, your photos or any health data from your phone.

## What stays on your phone

What you do in the app stays on your phone: the lessons you finished and your candles, your saved lessons, your answers to "How did that feel?", everything you write in the Journal, and your settings. None of it is sent anywhere. **You → Delete my data** erases it, and so does uninstalling the app.

If backup is switched on in your phone's settings, Android may include this app's data in your phone's own backup to your Google account. That backup is yours: we cannot see it.

## Your journal

What you write in the Journal is kept only on this phone. It is never sent anywhere, and nobody, Maria included, can read it. The weekly look-backs in the Habit and Health journals are worked out on this phone, for you alone: nothing you write is counted or analysed anywhere else. The Health journal never asks about weight, calories or body measurements. If you choose **You → Export my journal**, the app hands the text to the app you pick, such as your email or your notes: from there, that app's own rules apply.

## What the app downloads

The lessons, pictures and notes, and the small page that holds the video player, are downloaded from GitHub Pages, a website service run by GitHub. Like any website, it receives your phone's internet address when the app asks for them, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Videos

Lesson videos are played with YouTube's player. When you play a video, YouTube (a Google service) receives the request as it would on its own website, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## No ads, no tracking

The app shows no advertising and contains no analytics or tracking tools.

## Children

The app is intended for adults.
